Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SUKOON Wellness handles information shared through our website and communication channels.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SUKOON Wellness. We respect your privacy and aim to provide transparency regarding the information that may be shared when you visit our website or contact us.

2. Information You May Share

When you contact SUKOON Wellness through WhatsApp, phone, or other communication channels, you may voluntarily share information such as your name, phone number or messages.

Please share only information that you are comfortable providing.

3. Website Information

Our website may automatically receive limited technical information such as browser type, device information or general website usage data for basic website functionality and improvement.

4. How Information May Be Used

Information voluntarily shared with us may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to communication or enquiries.
  • Improving communication and website experience.
  • Maintaining the safety and proper functioning of our platform.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations when required.

5. Third-Party Services

SUKOON Wellness may use third-party communication platforms such as WhatsApp. When you use these services, their respective privacy policies and terms may also apply.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party platforms they choose to use.

6. Data Security

We aim to take reasonable steps to maintain appropriate security. However, no online communication or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

SUKOON Wellness is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent or legal basis.

8. Your Choices

You may choose whether or not to contact us and what information you wish to share. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information unless necessary and appropriate.

9. Changes to This Policy

SUKOON Wellness may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
